## 📸 College Photos & Memories Gallery

A collection dedicated to preserving the memorable moments, friendships, campus experiences, and special occasions from **Annai Theresa Arts and Science College**.

### What This Gallery Is For

- **Campus Memories** — Photos of the college campus and memorable places.
- **Friends & Classmates** — Group photos and moments shared with classmates.
- **College Events** — Photographs from celebrations, cultural programmes, and other events, when available.
- **Everyday College Life** — Classroom moments, campus gatherings, and everyday experiences.
- **Special Memories** — Farewell moments, achievements, and other meaningful occasions.

### 🖼️ Photo Gallery

Add your original college photographs to the repository and display them here.

Suggested image organization:

```text
college-memories/
├── README.md
├── index.html
└── images/
    └── gallery/
        ├── campus.jpg
        ├── classmates.jpg
        ├── college-event.jpg
        └── farewell.jpg
```

*The filenames above are examples. Replace them with the actual photographs and filenames you add to the repository.*

Once the images have been uploaded, you can display them in this README:

```md
![College Campus](images/gallery/campus.jpg)
![Classmates](images/gallery/classmates.jpg)
![College Event](images/gallery/college-event.jpg)
![Farewell Memories](images/gallery/farewell.jpg)
```

### ❤️ Preserving Our Memories

Every photograph captures a moment, and every moment becomes part of our college story. This project is intended to help preserve and revisit the memories associated with college life.

*Please upload photographs only when you have permission to share them, especially when other students appear in the images.*
